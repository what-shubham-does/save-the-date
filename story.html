Our Story So Far

How We Got Here

The slightly improbable sequence of events that led two people, a Hinge match, and a rained-on scooter ride all the way to a temple in Goa.

There were, perhaps, signs.

A Hinge match. A visit to Pune. A café that happened to be closed for renovations. A nearby pub that happened to be starting a quiz just as we arrived.

The quiz revealed a shared weakness for trivia and completely unnecessary facts. The next date revealed that twelve hours could disappear surprisingly quickly when you combine the Aga Khan Palace, a terrible movie, strong drinks, and dinner.

Then, the night before Anmol was due to leave Pune, she mentioned she would like to see Shubham once more.

So he rode 20 km through the rain. They ate waffles on the steps of a closed bank and talked late into the evening.

Apparently, that was enough to make five months of long distance seem like a good idea.

It was. We made things official in December, and two years brought many trips, countless conversations, and plenty more reasons to keep choosing each other.

Eventually, Shubham planned a walking tour of CSMT and proposed on the terrace — an appropriately architectural gesture for a conservation architect.

✦

She still does the accents and voices he loves.
He still listens to three-hour podcasts at completely unreasonable hours.

So perhaps the signs were right.

We'd love for you to join us as we celebrate the next chapter of our story — with the people who have made our lives happier along the way.
