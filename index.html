02 · Getting Here

Finding your way to Mardol

✈️

By Air

Two airports serve Goa

Dabolim (GOI) is the closest — ~30 km, ~45 min. Pre-paid taxis have fixed rates.

Mopa (GOX) is newer and further (~65 km). Good if spending days up north first.

🚂

By Train

Konkan Railway

Karmali (Old Goa) is nearest, ~18 km. Taxi or auto from there.

Margao and Vasco da Gama have more connections — both ~25–35 km away.

🚗

By Road

Self-drive or hired car

Mardol sits on NH 4A — well-signposted and GPS-friendly. Use the Maps links in the venue section above.

Driving from Mumbai or Pune? NH 66 along the coast is genuinely beautiful.

🏨

Where to Stay

Nearby options

Ponda town has comfortable guesthouses nearby. Panaji (~22 km) and South Goa beaches (~30–40 km) offer more range. Book early — December is peak season.

✦

03 · Getting Around

Moving around Goa

A quick honest word: Goa doesn't have a metro or reliable ride-hailing apps. But it has character — and several perfectly workable ways to get around.

TaxisMost comfortableAlways fix the fare before you set off — taxis don't use meters. Airport pre-paid taxis have printed fixed rates; use these on arrival.
Scooter rentalMost freedomThe classic Goa move. ₹300–500/day. Carry your licence, inspect the bike for scratches, and always wear a helmet — NH 4A moves fast.
PilotsFastest locallyGoa's motorcycle taxis — spotted by yellow mudguards. Fast and cheap for short hops. One pillion only. Agree the fare upfront.
KTC BusesBudgetKadamba Transport Corporation covers most major routes including Ponda. Tickets ₹10–15. Buses stop around 7 pm.
Auto-rickshawsIn-town hopsYellow-and-black three-wheelers in Ponda town. Good for short distances. Fix the fare before you get in.

04 · Explore

Things to do in Goa

Whether you're here for a day or a week, Goa has many faces. Here are some worth seeking out.

Right around the venue
—
On-site
Temple

Shri Mahalasa Narayani Temple

The temple you'll be celebrating in. A 450-year-old sanctuary dedicated to Goddess Mahalasa. Walk through at your own pace — the deepstambha and inner mandapa are especially beautiful.

2
km
Temple

Shri Manguesh Temple, Mangueshi

One of Goa's oldest temples, dedicated to Lord Shiva. Whitewashed façade, ornamental lamp tower, large temple pond. Best visited early morning.

8
km
Spice Farm

Sahakari Spice Farm, Ponda

A working plantation with guided walks through cardamom, pepper, vanilla, and nutmeg. Goan thali lunch under the trees. A lovely, slow half-day.

05 · Eat

Where to eat well

Goan food is its own reason to visit. Don't leave without a proper fish curry-rice thali, and save room for bebinca.

Savor Restaurant & Bar

Goan & SeafoodNear the TempleMid-range

A local favourite for post-temple lunches. Try the Mackerel Rechado Masala or Prawns Curry Rice. Open from 12:30 pm.

Tilve Bhojanalay

Authentic GoanSeafood ThaliBudget

No-frills, homestyle, outstanding value. Famous for Pomfret Rava Fry and an affordable seafood thali.

Pan Aroma Restaurant

Goan & North IndianPonda TownFamily-friendly

Solid spread of dishes, good for groups. Their butter garlic prawns are a reliable crowd-pleaser.

Cool 'n' Spice

Multi-cuisinePonda

Relaxed and good for an evening meal. Try the Chicken Cafreal — Goa's green-spiced grilled chicken at its best.

A brief guide to Goan cuisine

Look out for: Fish Curry Rice (the everyday staple), Prawn Balchão (fiery and pickled), Chicken Xacuti (complex coconut-and-spice curry), and Bebinca — a layered coconut-milk dessert. Order it wherever you see it.
